# Gesture Commands

## Unreleased

First release.

- Tap, hold and long-hold gestures for anything on the gamepad crossbar: a gesture attaches to a spell or
  item on a crossbar slot (or a macro that shows one); a tap does what the slot normally does, a hold and
  a longer hold cast what you assign.
- Hold actions can be spells, items, toys, mounts or macros.
- Gestures are macros for your gamepad: each has a name and icon (edited with the macro icon picker) and is
  stored as a character macro you can place on the crossbar.
- Setup window (`/gestures`, or Options > AddOns): drag and drop actions in, Place on bar / drag gestures
  out, hold timing; works with Forever's controller navigation.
- `/gestures on|off|test|debug|hold <n>|hold2 <n>`.
